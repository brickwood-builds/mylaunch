Welcome to MyLaunch

Your personal shortcut to everything you use every day. Here's how to get started:

  • Click a tile to open that link in a new tab
  • Add a section to group your links, then click Add link to fill it in
  • Click + next to Home to create more screens — Work, Hobbies, anything you like
  • Hover a tile and click the three dots to quickly edit or remove it
  • Hit Edit to add links, create sections, rename, reorder or remove anything
  • Drag tiles between sections to reorganize in edit mode
  • S / M / L controls the icon size — pick what works for you

💡 Tip — take it everywhere: Click Sign in to sync at the top and your links follow you to any device. Set this page as your browser homepage and you're all set.
